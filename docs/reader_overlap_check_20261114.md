# Reader overlap between ZuCo 1.0 and 2.0

14 November 2026. Reading only. Nothing was downloaded, trained or scored, and no EEG file was opened.

**Finding: the dataset's own authors state that the two releases have different participants.** This is published evidence, not a direct confirmation from the first author, who has not replied to the owner's email of 29 October.

## The statement

[Hollenstein, Tröndle, Plomecka, Kiegeland, Özyurt, Jäger and Langer, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06310), "Reading Task Classification Using EEG and Eye-Tracking Data", Section 2. The authors include the first and senior authors of both dataset papers.

- "We recorded two separate datasets with different participants."
- "ZuCo includes high-density EEG and eye-tracking data of 30 healthy adult native English speakers."

30 is 12 plus 18, which also fits no overlap. Both quotes were checked against the paper's text, not only a summary.

## A consistency check from ages and sex

The dataset papers list each reader's age and sex ([ZuCo 1.0](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6289117/), Table 2; [ZuCo 2.0](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1912.00903), Table 1). Assuming a ZuCo 2.0 reader would be the same age or up to 3 years older:

- **11 of the 18 ZuCo 2.0 readers have no possible match** of the same sex and a fitting age.
- **7 have a possible match on age and sex:** YRH, YRK, YDG, YDR, YFR, YIS and YSD.
- **Other details differ but do not rule anyone out:** all 12 ZuCo 1.0 readers are right-handed, and ZuCo 2.0 includes two left-handed readers, not identified.

This check cannot prove overlap or its absence. It only shows that the published details do not contradict the authors' statement. The 3-year window is an assumption, as the recording dates are not compared here.

## What this changes

- **Cross-release results:** they can be described as using different people, citing this statement, rather than as "participant overlap unknown".
- **Wording to keep:** the evidence is a published statement by the dataset authors, not a subject-level mapping. The paper should cite it as such.
- **Not changed:** the transfer runs still include sentences shared by both releases, so they remain exploratory for that reason.
- **The email:** no reply is needed for the audit paper. A reply would only add confirmation.

Sources searched: the two dataset papers, the 2021 paper above, and one web search for the first author's thesis, which was not found. A [2026 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.21847) was reported to state no participant overlap, but its abstract page does not, so it is not used.
